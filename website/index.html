the stuff it won't brag about

Quietly capable.

Everything below happens only when someone actually asks. No announcements, no menus. Just say it in plain language and it handles it.

🌀

Keeps the group alive

When the chat's gone quiet for days, it drops an unprompted hot take or a bit of gossip to get everyone talking again. Only when the room actually needs it.

🔥

Roasts on request

"Roast Dan for being late again." It obliges — affectionately savage, never mean enough to start a real fight. Brave enough? Tell it to roast you.

🍵

News & gossip, curated

Ask what's going on and it serves the headlines that actually matter to your group — plus the lighter stuff that's just fun to talk about.

…and once it's earned its place, the genuinely useful stuff 👇
📍

Place lookups

"Find us a nice restaurant on the beach in Vourvourou, Greece." It comes back with the spot, its hours, phone, and what it's known for.

👤

People lookups

Wondering who someone is? Ask about a name and it gives you the quick rundown — who they are and why they're worth knowing.

🔍

Looks things up

Searches the web and reads the links you paste — then gives you the short version, not a 12-paragraph essay.

⏰

Reminders that stick

"Remind us every Monday to send the report." One-off or recurring — daily, weekly, monthly. It pings the group right on time.

🗳️

Polls, settled

"Pizza or sushi?" It spins up a real poll, tallies the votes, and tells you who's still on the fence.

✅

Action items & tasks

Assign who's bringing what, track what's done, and ask "what's still open?" without scrolling back 200 messages.

🖼️

Images on demand

Generate a picture from a description, or edit the last one someone sent — "make the sky blue" — and drop it back in the chat.

💸

Splits the bill (and the awkwardness)

"Split 340 lei between 5 of us, add a 10% tip." Done, with names, before anyone reaches for the calculator app.

Adding it to a group

01

Start the chat

Scan the QR or tap Add Gepetel to a group to open a chat — then save it to your contacts as "Gepetel" so you can add it to any group.

02

Add it to your group

Open the WhatsApp group → Group info → Add member → pick Gepetel. It'll say a quick, slightly ironic hello.

03

Forget it's there

It stays quiet until someone needs something. Then you just ask, in normal words, and it handles it. No commands to memorize.

# Google verification video, recorded on your phone

Google wants every permission (gmail.modify, calendar.events) shown working, the consent screen with every permission in full, and each change appearing in your real Gmail and Calendar. Two takes, each about 3 minutes, no editing needed:

- **Video 1, Gmail:** steps 1–6, then 10.
- **Video 2, Calendar:** steps 1–2 again (connect from scratch, consent fully expanded), then 7–10.

Each video starts with the consent screen, because a reviewer may watch only one of them. Disconnect Google between the two takes, so the second one starts from scratch too.

## Before you record

- **Phone language: English.** The reviewers read the consent screen.
- **Use a test Google account** that's a test user of the "gepetel" project, so the consent screen is the one under review. The account should have:
  - 3–4 ordinary emails in the inbox, one of them from "Ana" about "Friday dinner"
  - one newsletter
  - an empty calendar tomorrow
- **Disconnect Google in Gepetel first** (say "disconnect Google"), so the video starts from scratch.
- **Do Do Not Disturb**, so no notifications pop up mid-take.
- **Start screen recording with the microphone on:** open Control Center, long-press the record button, and turn Microphone on. You narrate the lines in *italics* as you go. Speaking is enough; you don't need to type them.
- **Have three apps ready:** WhatsApp (open on the chat with Gepetel), Gmail, and Google Calendar.
- **Do one practice run** without recording, so each step works on the first try.

## The take

### 1. What this is (WhatsApp, 15 s)
*"This is Gepetel, an assistant that lives in WhatsApp. I'll connect my Google account and show everything it does with Gmail and Google Calendar."*

### 2. Consent: every permission in full (1 min)
- Send to Gepetel: **Connect my Google account**
- Tap the link it sends you.
- On Google's screen, show that the app is **Gepetel**, then tap **"Show all"** / expand every permission. Scroll slowly so both can be read:
  - "Read, compose, and send emails…" (gmail.modify)
  - "View and edit events on all your calendars" (calendar.events)
- *"Gepetel asks for exactly these two permissions: Gmail modify and Calendar events."*
- Tap **Allow**, then go back to WhatsApp and show Gepetel saying it's connected.

### 3. Gmail: reading (45 s)
- Send: **What's new in my inbox?** Gepetel lists the emails.
- Switch to the **Gmail app** and show the same emails.
- *"Reading needs Gmail access. It only reads what I ask about."*

### 4. Gmail: replying, which is a write (45 s)
- Send: **Reply to Ana's email about Friday dinner: "Sounds good, see you at 8."**
- Gepetel shows the reply and asks you to confirm. Answer **yes**.
- In **Gmail, open Sent** and show the reply there.
- *"Sending happens only after I say yes, and the reply shows up in my Gmail."*

### 5. Gmail: labelling and archiving, a modify (45 s)
- Send: **Archive the newsletter and label it "Later".**
- In **Gmail**, show it's gone from the Inbox, then open the **"Later" label** and show it there.
- *"This is why Gepetel needs modify rather than read-only: it files mail for me."*

### 6. Gmail: deleting, which moves to Trash (30 s)
- Send: **Delete the newsletter.** Confirm with **yes** if asked.
- In **Gmail, open Trash** and show it there.
- *"Delete means Trash. Gepetel never deletes permanently."*

### 7. Calendar: reading (30 s)
- Send: **What's on my calendar this week?**
- Show the same week in **Google Calendar**.

### 8. Calendar: create, move, cancel (1 min 30 s)
- Send: **Add "Dentist" tomorrow at 10:00.** Show it in **Calendar**.
- Send: **Move the dentist to 11:00.** Show it moved in **Calendar**.
- Send: **Cancel the dentist.** Show it gone from **Calendar**.
- *"Creating, moving and cancelling events needs calendar.events. That's all it can change: the events, not my calendars or their settings."*

### 9. Why not narrower permissions (15 s)
*"Read-only scopes couldn't send, file, delete or change events, and Gepetel does all of these, always on my request."*

### 10. Disconnect (30 s)
- Send: **Disconnect Google.** Gepetel confirms.
- Open **myaccount.google.com/permissions** in the browser and show that Gepetel is no longer listed.
- *"One message and Gepetel's access is gone."*

Stop recording.

You read one WhatsApp message an assistant is about to send. Decide one thing: does it TELL them the assistant is doing something right now, or is about to, in place of the result? Examples of yes: "I'm checking your order now", "let me look that up", "one moment", "I'll track it and keep you posted", "verific acum", "mă uit și revin", "o clipă, caut".

It is "no" when the message:
- gives an answer or a result;
- ASKS them something — including asking whether they want it done ("want me to check?", "vrei să mă uit?"): a question is never a yes;
- says what it cannot do, or confirms something already done;
- says a job was started and its result will arrive by itself ("am pornit…, îți scriu când e gata").

Answer with one word: yes or no.

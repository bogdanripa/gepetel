You read one WhatsApp message an assistant is about to send, and what the person had written to it. The assistant ran NO tool in this turn: it looked nothing up, set nothing, changed nothing, sent nothing. Decide which of three things the message is.

doing — it TELLS them the assistant is doing something right now, or is about to, in place of the result: "I'm checking your order now", "let me look that up", "one moment", "I'll track it and keep you posted", "verific acum", "mă uit și revin", "o clipă, caut".

A message often has several parts. Judge the part that speaks of an action: if ANY sentence in the assistant's own voice announces work or claims an action, that decides it, whatever the rest of the message is — "You're right, it's Ilinca, not Irina. I've corrected the reminder too" is done, even though it opens by agreeing with them.

done — it says the assistant HAS just done something that takes an action outside this message: set, moved, corrected or cancelled a reminder; added or changed a calendar event; posted or sent something to someone; saved, deleted or connected something. "Am corectat reminderul", "gata, am pus în calendar", "I've passed it on", "am setat", "l-am mutat pe mâine", "done — sent". Since no tool ran, none of that happened.

no — everything else. In particular it is "no" when the message:
- gives an answer, an explanation or a result of thinking (a translation, a rewritten text, advice, a calculation);
- IS a text written for them to send to someone else, whatever that text says ("...am rezervat data cu Tania" in a message they asked for is their sentence, not the assistant's claim) — when they asked for a message, a reply, a translation or a text put right, what comes back is that text;
- ASKS them something — including asking whether they want it done ("want me to check?", "vrei să mă uit?"): a question is never doing or done;
- says what it cannot do, or that something is NOT done yet;
- tells THEM what to do, step by step — even when it adds what the assistant will do once they have ("add my number to the group and I'll say hello there");
- says a job was started and its result will arrive by itself ("am pornit…, îți scriu când e gata");
- only acknowledges ("ok", "am înțeles", "bine, aștept", "noted") or says it will bear something in mind in this conversation.

Answer with one word: doing, done or no.

First, who is this? Look at how they use him. Someone who chats with him, tells
him about their day, asks him to help them get ready for something — a nudge
from him is natural. Someone who uses him as a reference desk — one factual
question after another, never a word about themselves, never an answer to
anything he asked — is not waiting to hear from him, and a "how did it go?" to
them is a stranger tapping on the window. For that person the answer is NO
unless they said, in so many words, that they are about to do a thing that
matters to them.

Answer NO for everything else, and NO is the commoner answer:
 - anything about HIM or about using him: whether they managed to add him to a
   group, whether connecting their mail or calendar worked, whether his own
   messages or reminders arrived, whether a link he sent opened. He can see
   those things himself, and asking is an advertisement dressed as concern;
 - anything he has already set a reminder for, or is already keeping an eye on
   for them: that IS the coming back;
 - an errand read off a lookup: they asked a shop's opening hours, a train time,
   a price, a phone number, what a document is for. That they might go there is
   not a plan they shared — "how was your visit to the shop?" is the question
   nobody answers;
 - a message he wrote FOR them to send to someone (a teacher, a client, a
   friend). What came of it is between them and that person;
 - a child's homework, a worksheet, a translation, a text he corrected;
 - a problem of theirs he did NOT solve. If something they asked for is still
   broken, the only thing worth coming back with is the fix;
 - small talk, jokes, a greeting, a bare "mersi";
 - a fact, a spelling, a translation, a definition, a sum — looked up and done
   with, with nothing of theirs happening next;
 - how something works, what something costs, what something is;
 - a product, a price or a model he helped them choose, unless they have said
   they are getting it, or doing something with it, and roughly when. "Dă-mi un
   model de schiuri pe care să îl cumpăr" is research, not an event — someone
   comparing things is not someone with a plan;
 - a problem he diagnosed for them (a setting, a bug, a duplicate photo) where
   the fix is immediate and there is nothing to come back to;
 - anything where coming back would only be checking whether they liked his
   answer, or a way of keeping a quiet chat going.

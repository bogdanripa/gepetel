# GEP-114 — A bulk "remind me before each activity" covers the whole schedule, and the reply says how many were set

Related: GEP-113 (daily review for 2026-11-20, where this was found).

## What happened
In a DM (2026-11-19), a person sent a picture of her children's schedule for **20 Nov – 5 Dec**
(about 22 activities with clear start times) and asked for a reminder one hour before each.
Gepetel answered: *"Am setat remindere cu o oră înainte pentru activitățile din program ale căror
ore sunt clare."*

The `reminders` collection shows **6** reminders created, all due 20–24 Nov. Nothing for
25 Nov – 5 Dec. The reply claimed full coverage; the tool calls covered about a third.

The next day, after he really had recreated a reminder (a new row at 07:00:47Z), he apologised
for having "said I corrected the reminder without checking". That apology was false too. Both
mistakes have the same cause: his account of what he did did not come from what the tools returned.

## Why it matters
She relies on these reminders to get three children to their activities. A gap that shows up a
week later, after he said everything was set, is how she stops relying on him. She has already
said so ("Pai daca imi faci din astea nu ma mai bazez pe tine").

## What the code does today (read, not measured)
- DM `set_reminder` (`src/oai.ts`, `REMINDER_TOOLS`) takes **one** `{what, at, by_call?}` per call.
- A reply may take at most `MAX_TOOL_ROUNDS = 6` rounds (`src/toolLoop.ts`). 22 reminders need the
  model to keep issuing calls (or to batch them in parallel) and it stopped partway.
- When two or more reminders are set in one turn, `u.reminderReceipt` appends the exact list under
  the reply. That list is built from tool results, but the sentence above it is the model's own,
  and nothing tells the reader how many were asked for versus how many were set.

## Goal
One request for "a reminder before each of these" sets all of them in one step, and what he tells
the person about it (count, date range, anything skipped) comes from what was actually stored.

## Behaviour
1. **One call can set many reminders.** DM `set_reminder` accepts a list of reminders
   (each `{what, at, by_call?}`) in a single call. The single-reminder form keeps working.
   A schedule of at least 40 items fits in one call. A list that is longer than the limit is not
   cut off silently: the items over the limit come back as skipped, with that reason.
2. **Each item stands on its own.** An item with a time that doesn't parse, or one already in the
   past, is skipped with its reason. The other items are still set. One bad item never fails the
   whole call.
3. **No duplicates on a retry.** An item that matches a reminder already pending in this chat
   (same time, same text) is not created twice. It is reported as already set, so asking again
   after a partial run fills the gaps instead of doubling what was already there.
4. **The result says what happened.** The tool result gives the number set, the first and last
   time set, and each skipped item with its reason. It tells the model to state the count and
   the range from these numbers and to name anything skipped. It must not suggest coverage it
   does not have ("toate", "all of them") unless nothing was skipped.
5. **The count is in the message, written by code.** When reminders are set in a turn, the
   receipt under the reply starts with a line built from the stored rows, in the person's
   language: for example *"22 de remindere setate, între joi 20 nov și vineri 5 dec"*, followed
   by the existing list, then a line per skipped item and why. The model's own sentence can't
   contradict it, because the number is not the model's.
6. **Instructions for schedules.** The DM prompt tells him: when someone asks for a reminder for
   each item of a schedule, put every item in one `set_reminder` call. If some items have no
   clear time, set the rest and ask about those by name. Don't pick a subset on his own.
7. **What he says he did comes from the record.** When the person asks whether something was set,
   or says it wasn't, he checks (`list_my_reminders`) before he confirms or apologises. He never
   apologises for something the record shows he did. He never claims something the record
   doesn't show.
8. **Phone-call reminders in a list** follow the same rules as one: `by_call` is honoured only
   where a call can be placed, and the result says so per item.

## Acceptance criteria
- A single `set_reminder` call with 22 items creates 22 rows in `reminders` for that chat, due
  at the given local times (checked against the chat's timezone).
- With 22 items of which 2 are in the past and 1 has an unparseable time, 19 rows are created
  and the result names the 3 skipped items and why.
- Calling it again with the same 22 items creates no new rows for the 19 already pending, and
  creates the ones that were missing.
- The message the person receives contains a code-built count line whose number equals the rows
  created (or already pending) in that turn, plus the date range, plus the skipped items.
- A single-reminder call behaves exactly as today (same row, same result fields, no count line
  for one reminder unless the design decides one helps).
- Replay cases in `replays/` (names, children and dates changed):
  a. A picture-style schedule of ~20 activities over two weeks, "remind me an hour before each"
     → every activity is set in one call, and the reply does not claim more than was set.
  b. The person says "you didn't fix that reminder" when the record shows it was recreated
     → he checks, then says it is set. He does not apologise for something he didn't do.
- Unit tests cover the list handling (limits, skips, duplicates) and the count line.
- `node dist/replay.js` passes on the models in use before the prompt change ships.

## Out of scope
- How well vision reads the schedule picture (wrong times read off the image is a separate issue).
- Recurring reminders ("every Wednesday").
- The group `create_reminder` tool and the user-MCP `set_reminder` (`src/usermcp/tools.ts`).
  They can take the same list form later. This issue is the DM.
- Changing `MAX_TOOL_ROUNDS`.
- Backfilling the person's missing reminders for 25 Nov – 5 Dec. That is a one-off for a human to
  decide; this issue makes the next request work.

## Design

Backend and prompt only. No schema change (the `Reminder` model already has everything), no
frontend. Four pieces: a pure planner, the list form of DM `set_reminder`, a code-built count line
in the receipt, and the prompt rules with two replay cases.

### How this ships and is checked (no staging)
`main` is both the development and the production branch, and every push to `main` deploys the
Pi (`.github/workflows/deploy.yml` → `gepetel-coolify.bogdanripa.com`). There is **no staging**,
and one is not worth building for this: a second Gepetel needs its own WhatsApp number, its own
gateway session and its own keys, and this change is fully testable without one. So:
- Before merge: unit tests (`node scripts/test-changed.mjs`), the DB tests with
  `TEST_DATABASE_URL`, and `node dist/replay.js` with `OPENAI_API_KEY` — the replay is the
  pre-production gate for the prompt.
- After merge (QA, on production): confirm the running image is the merge commit, re-run the
  replay cases inside the deployed container, and run the DB acceptance script against a
  throwaway chat id (see the QA task). Nobody sends real reminders to a real person to test this.
- `staging_url` stays unset on purpose.

### 1. `src/util.ts` — `planReminders`, pure
```ts
export const MAX_REMINDERS_PER_CALL = 50;
type ReminderAsk = { what?: unknown; at?: unknown; by_call?: unknown };
type Pending = { reminder_id: string; title: string; due_date: Date | string };
export type SkipReason = "no_text" | "time_unparsed" | "in_past" | "over_limit";
export function planReminders(asks: ReminderAsk[], o: { timezone: string; now: Date; pending: Pending[]; limit?: number }): {
  create:  { i: number; what: string; at: Date; by_call: boolean }[];
  already: { i: number; what: string; at: Date; reminder_id: string }[];
  skipped: { i: number; what: string; at: string; reason: SkipReason }[];
}
```
- `i` is the item's 0-based index in the call, so the model can match results to its list.
- Order of checks per item: index ≥ `limit` → `over_limit` (never cut silently); empty `what` →
  `no_text`; `localTimeToDate(at, timezone)` null → `time_unparsed`; `< now + 60s` → `in_past`
  (the same threshold the single form uses today).
- **Duplicates.** A key is `minute(due_date) + "|" + foldText(what)`, where `foldText` lowercases,
  strips diacritics (NFKD, drop `\p{M}`), collapses whitespace and trims trailing punctuation —
  so "Înot Ana" and "inot ana." match, and two different activities at the same hour do not.
  - Against `pending` (this chat's future reminders): a match goes to `already` with the existing
    `reminder_id`, nothing is created. `by_call` differences don't make it a new reminder.
  - Within the call: the second copy of the same key also goes to `already`, pointing at the
    first. A schedule listed twice is not two reminders.
- No I/O, no `Date.now()` inside — `now` is passed in, so it is unit-testable.

### 2. `src/mongo.ts` — pending reminders without the 50 cap
- `pendingReminders(chatId)`: `Reminder.find({ chat_id, due_date: { $gt: new Date() } }, { reminder_id: 1, title: 1, due_date: 1 }).lean()` — no limit (`listReminders` caps at 50, and a
  dedup that can't see row 51 creates a duplicate). Export on the default object.

### 3. `src/oai.ts` — DM `set_reminder` takes a list
Schema (`REMINDER_TOOLS[0]`), still `strict: false`:
```jsonc
{ "what": "...", "at": "YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM", "by_call": false,          // the single form, as today
  "items": [ { "what": "...", "at": "YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM", "by_call": false }, ... ] }  // the list form
```
- `required` becomes `[]`; `items` has `maxItems` **omitted** (the limit is enforced in code so the
  overflow is reported, not rejected by the schema). Its description: "Several reminders at once —
  every item of a schedule, in ONE call. Up to 50."
- **Single form** (no `items`, or `items` empty): the current branch, untouched — same row, same
  result object, same `setNow` push. No dedup there: it behaves exactly as today.
- **List form** (`items` is a non-empty array; if `what`/`at` are also given they are added as the
  first item): `pending = await m.pendingReminders(userId)`, `plan = u.planReminders(...)`.
  - If any `create` item has `by_call`, call `voice.canCallThem` **once** and apply the verdict to
    every such item (same `byCall` rule as the single form).
  - Create the rows one by one with `m.toolFunctions.create_reminder` (≤50 saves). A save that
    throws turns that item into a skip with reason `"not_saved"` and the rest continue — one bad
    item never fails the call.
  - Push every created **and** already-set item to `setNow` (`{ at, what, already?: true }`, the
    📞 prefix as today); push every skip to a new `skippedNow: { what, at, reason }[]`.
  - Result (the numbers the model must use; all times local `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM` + ISO):
    ```jsonc
    { "set": 22, "created": 19, "already_set": 3, "skipped_count": 3,
      "first": "2026-11-20 15:00", "last": "2026-12-05 09:00",
      "results": [ { "i": 0, "status": "created", "reminder_id": "…", "at": "…", "what": "…" },
                   { "i": 4, "status": "already_set", "reminder_id": "…" },
                   { "i": 7, "status": "skipped", "reason": "in_past", "what": "…", "at": "…" },
                   { "i": 9, "status": "created", "by_call": false, "call": "You can NOT phone them (…)" } ],
      "tell_the_user": "…" }
    ```
    `set` = created + already_set (what is pending now). `tell_the_user` is built from the numbers:
    "State the count (N) and the range (first → last) from these numbers. Name every skipped item
    and why, and ask about the ones without a clear time by name. The exact list is added under
    your message by the system — don't write it." plus, **only when `skipped_count > 0`**: "Do NOT
    say all/toate/every one — K were not set." `by_call` per item follows the single form's wording.
- At the end of the turn: `u.reminderReceipt(setNow, timezone, language, skippedNow)`.

### 4. `src/util.ts` — the count line in `reminderReceipt`
`reminderReceipt(items: { at; what; already?: boolean }[], timezone, language = "English", skipped: { what; at: string; reason }[] = [])`
- **When there is a receipt:** two or more set (as today), **or** at least one skipped. One reminder
  set with nothing skipped → `""`, exactly as today (the model's one line is enough and the single
  form must not change). One set + one skipped → a receipt, because the skip is the thing the
  model's sentence leaves out.
- **Line 1, the count**, from `items` (never from the model):
  - Romanian: `"22 de remindere setate, între joi 20 nov. și vineri 5 dec."`; one day:
    `"3 remindere setate, joi 20 nov."`; one: `"1 reminder setat, joi 20 nov."`; none:
    `"Niciun reminder setat."`. Romanian numerals take **"de"** when the last two digits are 00 or
    20–99 (20 de, 101, 120 de).
  - English: `"22 reminders set, Thu 20 Nov – Fri 5 Dec"`, `"1 reminder set, Thu 20 Nov"`,
    `"No reminders set."`.
  - When some were already pending: append `" (3 erau deja setate)"` / `" (3 were already set)"`.
  - Dates via `Intl.DateTimeFormat(RECEIPT_LOCALE[language], { weekday: "short", day: "numeric", month: "short", timeZone })`. Words for other languages fall back to English; the dates stay in
    the locale.
- **Then** the existing `⏰` lines, unchanged, sorted by time.
- **Then** one line per skip: `⚠️ <what> (<at as given>) — <reason>`, reason words in ro/en:
  `in_past` "e deja în trecut" / "already past"; `time_unparsed` "ora nu e clară" / "no clear time";
  `over_limit` "peste limita de 50 dintr-o dată" / "over the limit of 50 at once";
  `no_text` "fără text" / "no text"; `not_saved` "nu s-a putut salva" / "could not be saved".
- Update `tests/oneToOneManners.test.mjs`: the existing two-item expectation gains the count line.

### 5. `prompts/dm.txt` — section 13
- Replace the "Several reminders at once" bullet's opening with spec item 6: when they ask for a
  reminder before each item of a schedule (a timetable, a screenshot, a list), put **every** item in
  **one** `set_reminder` call with `items`. Don't pick a subset. Items without a clear time: leave
  them out, set the rest, and ask about those by name. Keep the rest of that bullet (the system
  writes the list; one short line from him).
- Add spec item 7 next to the "Never say something is already set…" bullet: when they ask whether
  something was set, or say it wasn't ("nu l-ai corectat", "you didn't set it"), call
  `list_my_reminders` **before** confirming or apologising. If the record shows it, say it is set
  and when — no apology. Never claim what the record doesn't show.
- Asking again after a partial run: send the whole list again; what is already set comes back as
  `already_set` and is not doubled.

### 6. Replays (`replays/cases.json`, `src/replay.ts`)
- `judge` gains `itemsAtLeast: { set_reminder: 20 }`: passes when **one** `set_reminder` call
  carries at least that many items (`items.length`, or 1 for the single form). Unit-tested in
  `tests/replay.test.mjs` like the other expectations.
- Case a — "1:1: a two-week schedule, a reminder an hour before each": message is an `[image]`
  description listing ~20 activities over two weeks (names and dates invented) + "pune-mi
  reminder cu o oră înainte de fiecare". `toolResults.set_reminder`: a list result with
  `set: 18, skipped_count: 2` (two in the past). Expect `itemsAtLeast: { set_reminder: 20 }` and
  `no: ["toate", "pe toate"]` (2 were not set, so "all" is false).
- Case b — "1:1: told he didn't fix a reminder that the record shows he did": history has him
  cancelling and re-setting one; message "nu ai corectat reminderul"; `toolResults.list_my_reminders`
  shows the corrected row. Expect `calls: ["list_my_reminders"]`, `no: ["scuze", "îmi pare rău", "n-am verificat", "nu am verificat"]`.
- `node dist/replay.js` (all cases, models in use) must pass before the prompt PR merges.

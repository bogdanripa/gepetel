# GEP-114 — A bulk "remind me before each activity" covers the whole schedule, and the reply says how many were set

Related: GEP-113 (daily review for 2026-11-20, where this was found).

## What happened
In a DM (2026-11-19), a person sent a picture of her children's schedule for **20 Nov – 5 Dec**
(about 22 activities with clear start times) and asked for a reminder one hour before each.
Gepetel answered: *"Am setat remindere cu o oră înainte pentru activitățile din program ale căror
ore sunt clare."*

The `reminders` collection shows **6** reminders created, all due 20–24 Nov. Nothing for
25 Nov – 5 Dec. The reply claimed full coverage; the tool calls covered about a third.

The next day, after he really had recreated a reminder (a new row at 07:00:47Z), he apologised
for having "said I corrected the reminder without checking". That apology was false too. Both
mistakes have the same cause: his account of what he did did not come from what the tools returned.

## Why it matters
She relies on these reminders to get three children to their activities. A gap that shows up a
week later, after he said everything was set, is how she stops relying on him. She has already
said so ("Pai daca imi faci din astea nu ma mai bazez pe tine").

## What the code does today (read, not measured)
- DM `set_reminder` (`src/oai.ts`, `REMINDER_TOOLS`) takes **one** `{what, at, by_call?}` per call.
- A reply may take at most `MAX_TOOL_ROUNDS = 6` rounds (`src/toolLoop.ts`). 22 reminders need the
  model to keep issuing calls (or to batch them in parallel) and it stopped partway.
- When two or more reminders are set in one turn, `u.reminderReceipt` appends the exact list under
  the reply. That list is built from tool results, but the sentence above it is the model's own,
  and nothing tells the reader how many were asked for versus how many were set.

## Goal
One request for "a reminder before each of these" sets all of them in one step, and what he tells
the person about it (count, date range, anything skipped) comes from what was actually stored.

## Behaviour
1. **One call can set many reminders.** DM `set_reminder` accepts a list of reminders
   (each `{what, at, by_call?}`) in a single call. The single-reminder form keeps working.
   A schedule of at least 40 items fits in one call. A list that is longer than the limit is not
   cut off silently: the items over the limit come back as skipped, with that reason.
2. **Each item stands on its own.** An item with a time that doesn't parse, or one already in the
   past, is skipped with its reason. The other items are still set. One bad item never fails the
   whole call.
3. **No duplicates on a retry.** An item that matches a reminder already pending in this chat
   (same time, same text) is not created twice. It is reported as already set, so asking again
   after a partial run fills the gaps instead of doubling what was already there.
4. **The result says what happened.** The tool result gives the number set, the first and last
   time set, and each skipped item with its reason. It tells the model to state the count and
   the range from these numbers and to name anything skipped. It must not suggest coverage it
   does not have ("toate", "all of them") unless nothing was skipped.
5. **The count is in the message, written by code.** When reminders are set in a turn, the
   receipt under the reply starts with a line built from the stored rows, in the person's
   language: for example *"22 de remindere setate, între joi 20 nov și vineri 5 dec"*, followed
   by the existing list, then a line per skipped item and why. The model's own sentence can't
   contradict it, because the number is not the model's.
6. **Instructions for schedules.** The DM prompt tells him: when someone asks for a reminder for
   each item of a schedule, put every item in one `set_reminder` call. If some items have no
   clear time, set the rest and ask about those by name. Don't pick a subset on his own.
7. **What he says he did comes from the record.** When the person asks whether something was set,
   or says it wasn't, he checks (`list_my_reminders`) before he confirms or apologises. He never
   apologises for something the record shows he did. He never claims something the record
   doesn't show.
8. **Phone-call reminders in a list** follow the same rules as one: `by_call` is honoured only
   where a call can be placed, and the result says so per item.

## Acceptance criteria
- A single `set_reminder` call with 22 items creates 22 rows in `reminders` for that chat, due
  at the given local times (checked against the chat's timezone).
- With 22 items of which 2 are in the past and 1 has an unparseable time, 19 rows are created
  and the result names the 3 skipped items and why.
- Calling it again with the same 22 items creates no new rows for the 19 already pending, and
  creates the ones that were missing.
- The message the person receives contains a code-built count line whose number equals the rows
  created (or already pending) in that turn, plus the date range, plus the skipped items.
- A single-reminder call behaves exactly as today (same row, same result fields, no count line
  for one reminder unless the design decides one helps).
- Replay cases in `replays/` (names, children and dates changed):
  a. A picture-style schedule of ~20 activities over two weeks, "remind me an hour before each"
     → every activity is set in one call, and the reply does not claim more than was set.
  b. The person says "you didn't fix that reminder" when the record shows it was recreated
     → he checks, then says it is set. He does not apologise for something he didn't do.
- Unit tests cover the list handling (limits, skips, duplicates) and the count line.
- `node dist/replay.js` passes on the models in use before the prompt change ships.

## Out of scope
- How well vision reads the schedule picture (wrong times read off the image is a separate issue).
- Recurring reminders ("every Wednesday").
- The group `create_reminder` tool and the user-MCP `set_reminder` (`src/usermcp/tools.ts`).
  They can take the same list form later. This issue is the DM.
- Changing `MAX_TOOL_ROUNDS`.
- Backfilling the person's missing reminders for 25 Nov – 5 Dec. That is a one-off for a human to
  decide; this issue makes the next request work.
